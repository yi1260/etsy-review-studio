How to export Etsy reviews to CSV (free options first)

Etsy does have a free official review download, and it is the right place to start: Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → Take your data with you → Download Your Reviews. Open the file in a spreadsheet before you build anything around it, because Etsy does not publish the column list. If that file contains what you need, you are done. If it does not, this page covers the other routes and what each one quietly leaves behind.

Route 1 — Etsy's own download (free)

It is buried, which is why so many guides claim Etsy offers no review export at all. That claim is out of date. The download is under the "Take your data with you" section of Download Data, and it produces a file you own.

What to verify the moment you open it:

  1. The review body and date, in a parseable date format.
  2. Your own seller replies, if you wrote any.
  3. The original full-resolution buyer photo URLs, not resized preview URLs.
  4. A listing identifier or URL you can later map to a Shopify product handle.

Etsy's help article does not document these fields, so the only reliable check is opening the file yourself. If all four are present, a separate export tool may be unnecessary.

Route 2 — Judge.me's free Etsy sync (free, but not a file)

If you use Judge.me, its native Etsy integration is free and syncs on a schedule. It is the least-effort answer for a straightforward shop. It is not a CSV: the reviews land inside one app, and you cannot take a portable file away from it. It also has narrow coverage — active listings with written reviews, no seller replies, and no rating-only or inactive-listing reviews. See the dedicated limitations page for the full list.

Route 3 — a structured CSV you generate yourself

When the official file is missing columns, or when you need a portable snapshot, the manual route is to collect one row per review:

review_date, rating, reviewer_name, body, picture_urls, product_handle, seller_reply

Two details cause most failed imports:

  • Join on handle, not title. Etsy listing titles get renamed, abbreviated and translated. A Shopify handle is the stable key. Match the columns before you upload, and count unmatched rows — any unmatched row is a blocker, not a warning.
  • Keep the original listing URL. It is your only fallback when titles drift, and it is the closest thing to a record of which listing a review was actually written for.

A review attached to the wrong product is worse than no review at all.

A 5-row test import before you do 500

Pick five rows: one with a photo, one rating-only, one with a seller reply, one with a comma or line break in the body, and one from an older listing. Import into a test product in your review app. Check that the date renders in the right timezone, the photo loads at full resolution, the body is not truncated at the comma, and the review landed on the intended product. Re-run the same five rows — if you now have ten, you have no stable per-review identifier and need deduplication.

Keep the original file untouched as your archive.

The legal bit

Only import reviews written for your own products by your own customers. In the US, the FTC's Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, effective October 2024) prohibits presenting a review as if it were written for a product or seller it was not written for. Public reviews from other shops are useful for market research; they must never become your own storefront's social proof. Keep the Etsy attribution visible on the widget.

Frequently asked questions

Does Etsy have a built-in review export?

Yes. In Shop Manager go to Settings → Options → Download Data → Take your data with you → Download Your Reviews. Etsy does not publish the column list for that file, so open it and verify which fields you actually received.

Can I import Etsy reviews into Shopify for free?

Judge.me offers a free native Etsy sync. It covers written reviews on active listings, syncs on a schedule and does not produce a portable CSV. Check its coverage before relying on it for a full migration.

What columns does an Etsy review CSV need?

At minimum review_date, rating, reviewer_name, body, picture_urls, product_handle and seller_reply, plus a stable per-review identifier. Map product_handle to Shopify handles, never to listing titles.

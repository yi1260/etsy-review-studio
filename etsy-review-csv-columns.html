Etsy review CSV columns: 13 fields and the one join key that matters

The column that decides whether a Shopify import works is product_handle — the stable key you map to a Shopify product. Etsy attaches reviews to listings, not to products or variants, and listing titles get renamed. If you join on title, a rename silently drops or misattaches the review. Start with Etsy's free "Download Your Reviews" file (Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → Take your data with you → Download Your Reviews), open it, and compare what you actually got against the schema below.

If you verify only four things in any file — from Etsy, from a sync or from a tool — make them body plus review_date, seller_reply, full-resolution picture_urls, and the product_url / product_handle pair.

Why listings and products do not line up

Say one Etsy listing offers 20 designs and has 300 reviews, and on Shopify you want 20 separate products. The data was never recorded at that granularity, so no tool can cleanly decide which of the 300 belongs to which of the 20. Any claim to "split" them is a guess. What you can do is capture every review together with its original product_url and product_handle before you restructure, then assign the same handle or SKU on both sides from day one. Retrofitting 200 listings later is miserable.

Dates are the other quiet failure: store ISO 8601 with a timezone, and keep ratings numeric.

A five-row test import

  1. Pick five rows: one with a photo, one rating-only, one with a seller reply, one with a comma or line break in the body, one from an older listing.
  2. Import into a test product, not your live catalog.
  3. Check the timezone, full-resolution photo, untruncated body, and that the review attached to the intended product.
  4. Re-run the same five rows. If you now have ten, you have no stable transaction_id and need deduplication.
  5. Only then scale up. Keep the original file untouched as your archive.

The legal bit

Only import reviews genuinely written for your own products by your own customers. The FTC's Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, effective October 2024) prohibits misrepresenting a review's origin or the product it refers to. Public reviews from other shops are market research, not material for your storefront. Keep the Etsy attribution visible on the widget.

Frequently asked questions

Which CSV column maps Etsy reviews to Shopify products?

product_handle, mapped to the Shopify handle. Etsy attaches reviews to listings and listing titles get renamed, so joining on title silently breaks the import.

Why do my imported reviews attach to the wrong product?

The usual cause is joining on product_title, or missing a stable per-review identifier. Run a five-row test import into a test product and count unmatched rows before scaling up.

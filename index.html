The problem

Your reputation shouldn't live only inside someone else's dashboard.

Etsy does offer a reviews download — Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → Download Your Reviews — but its help article doesn't document which fields you actually get. If you're moving to your own storefront, archiving your shop history, or switching review apps, a file missing seller replies, original photo URLs or listing URLs is easy to discover too late.

1

Fields go undocumented

The official download exists, but Etsy doesn't publish its column list. Sellers often find out after migrating that seller replies or original photo URLs aren't in the file.

2

Syncs have edges

Free official syncs cover active listings with written reviews. Rating-only reviews, seller replies and inactive listings quietly fall outside that scope.

3

Research is manual

Finding which listings in a shop actually get talked about means clicking through pages and counting. There's no spreadsheet at the end of it.

Free, official, and worth doing first

How to download your Etsy reviews — and what to check in the file

You may not need any tool at all. Here's the first-party route, plus the four things sellers most often discover are missing only after a migration.

1

Open Shop Manager

Go to Settings → Options → Download Data. Etsy groups several exports here: orders, listings and, under "Take your data with you", Download Your Reviews.

2

Generate and open the file

Etsy prepares the download as a data request. Save it somewhere outside the browser's Downloads purgatory, then open it in a spreadsheet rather than assuming what's inside.

3

Check these four columns

Review body and date, your seller replies, the original full-resolution buyer photo URLs, and the listing URL each review belongs to. Etsy's help article doesn't document the field list, so verify on your own shop.

If those columns are all there and you only wanted a copy, you're done — no extension needed. If you need a repeatable, filterable snapshot with a documented schema, or you want to compare review volume across public shops, that's the case Review Studio was built for. Download requests also expire, so re-generate rather than relying on an old link.

See the exact 13 columns in the Standard CSV

These are the literal header names the extension writes. Copy them into your import mapping before you decide whether you need anything else.

transaction_id, review_date, rating, reviewer_name, body, photo_count, picture_urls, verified_buyer, product_id, product_handle, product_title, product_url, seller_reply

Judge.me and Loox column presets ship with Pro. The free tier exports the Standard CSV above, capped at 5 pages / 40 reviews per run.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

Is this an official Etsy or Judge.me tool?

No. Etsy Review Studio is an independent browser extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Etsy, Inc., Shopify Inc., Judge.me or Loox.

Do you upload or store my review data?

No. All extraction and CSV generation happen locally in your browser. Records and settings are cached only in chrome.storage.local on your device and never uploaded, tracked or sold. If you activate Pro, only the license key is sent to Gumroad for verification.

Doesn't Etsy already let me download my reviews?

Yes. Go to Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → Download Your Reviews. Try it first — for a plain copy of your own shop's data it may be all you need. Etsy's help article doesn't document the exact fields or format, so open the file and check for seller replies, original photo URLs and listing URLs. Review Studio is for when you need those fields in a predictable CSV, filters, or research across any public shop.

Why not just use Judge.me's free Etsy sync?

If you only need your own active, written reviews synced weekly into Judge.me, use it — it's the right tool. Review Studio covers what it doesn't: a complete portable CSV, seller replies, rating-only and inactive-listing reviews, a snapshot you can edit and keep, and public-shop research. It also works with Loox, or with no review app at all.

Can I use this to show other sellers' reviews in my store?

No. Public reviews are for research and category analysis. Displaying another seller's customers' feedback as your own is misleading. Review Studio is built to back up reviews you earned and to study public feedback — please keep attribution and only use reviews you are entitled to.

Will this cause problems with my Etsy account?

The extension reads public review pages from the tab you opened, with paced requests and automatic cooldowns. Use Safe mode for large shops and keep the built-in checkpoint resume on.

What if the CSV won't import into my review app?

Email yi1260ofhust@gmail.com with the app's CSV template. We'll check the column order and tell you whether a preset is needed, usually within 24 hours.

Privacy

Your review data never leaves your computer.

The extension reads publicly visible review pages from the Etsy tab you opened and writes a CSV to your device. Collected records and preferences are cached only in chrome.storage.local. Extraction and CSV generation happen entirely in the browser — there is no backend for review data, no account, no telemetry and no third-party analytics on this page.

If you activate Pro, only the license key is sent to Gumroad for verification. If you email support with a CSV template, that email is used solely to answer your question. Clearing the extension's local data — or removing the extension — deletes everything it stored.

Keep full-resolution Etsy buyer photo URLs, not thumbnails

Store the original full-resolution buyer photo URL, not the resized preview URL shown in a review widget. Preview URLs are derivatives: they are recompressed, often cropped, and can stop resolving once the original listing is gone. Start by opening Etsy's free "Download Your Reviews" file (Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → Take your data with you → Download Your Reviews) and checking whether it contains photo URLs at all — Etsy does not publish the column list for that file.

A photo URL is a reference, not a copy. If you only keep a downloaded file, you lose the ability to verify where it came from or recover it at full resolution.

How to store them in a CSV

Use a dedicated picture_urls column and a separate photo_count. If a review has several photos, keep them in one field with a consistent, documented separator, and say what that separator is in a note next to the file — otherwise the next person to open the CSV will guess.

review_date,rating,body,photo_count,picture_urls,product_url
2026-03-14,5,"Great print",2,"https://i.etsystatic.com/.../il_fullxfull.123.jpg;https://i.etsystatic.com/.../il_fullxfull.124.jpg",https://www.etsy.com/listing/123

Because URLs contain commas in query strings on some CDNs, quote the field. Check one row by opening the URL in a private browser window and confirming it resolves to the original-size image.

Verify after import, not before

A five-row test import should include one review with a photo and one with no photo. After importing, open the rendered review on a test product and check the image loads at full resolution rather than being re-fetched as a thumbnail by the review app. Some apps re-host images on their own CDN; that is their behaviour, not a reason to discard your original URLs — keep them in the archive.

The legal bit

Buyer photos are part of the review, and the same rule applies: only display photos attached to reviews written for your own products by your own customers. 16 CFR Part 465 (effective October 2024) prohibits presenting a review — including its media — as if it belonged to a product or seller it did not. Public photos from other shops are useful for market research; they are not assets for your product pages.

Frequently asked questions

Why do buyer photos look blurry after a migration?

A resized preview URL was usually copied instead of the original. Preview URLs are recompressed derivatives and may stop resolving after the listing is gone. Store the full-resolution URL in a dedicated picture_urls column.

Does Etsy’s official review download include photo URLs?

Etsy does not publish the column list for that file. Download it under Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data and open it to check.

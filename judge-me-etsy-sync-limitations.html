Judge.me’s free Etsy sync: the six things it doesn’t bring over

Judge.me shipped a free native Etsy → Shopify review sync in July 2025, and for a simple shop it is the right answer: connect your own Etsy shop, reviews sync on a schedule, photos come across, and they are marked verified. Try it before anything else. The reason people still end up needing a CSV is that a sync is a narrow projection, not a complete archive — here are the six boundaries worth knowing before you rely on it.

1. Only active listings with written reviews

Rating-only reviews are outside the sync. So are reviews attached to deactivated, sold-out, draft or expired listings. If you restructure a catalog, reviews on the listings you retire do not come along.

2. It only connects your own Etsy shop

That is by design and is the correct legal boundary. It is not a way to import another seller's reviews — that would be a misrepresentation, not a migration.

3. Seller replies are not imported

Your public response to a review is conversational context, and it is usually the first field lost in a migration. Once the original listing is gone, that text is not recoverable from the storefront.

4. Synced reviews cannot be edited, moved or deleted in Judge.me

They are locked to where the sync placed them. If an auto-match attached a review to the wrong product, you cannot simply move it inside the app. Check your listing mapping before the first sync; mapping changes going forward only affect future reviews.

5. Etsy-synced reviews are excluded from the Google Shopping ratings feed

If product ratings in Google Shopping matter to you, this is a real limitation, not a cosmetic one.

6. It does not give you a portable file

The reviews live inside Judge.me. Switch review apps, close the shop, or want an offline archive for an audit and you are back to square one. There is no CSV to keep.

What to do with this

Use the free sync for what it is good at — automatic, weekly, zero manual work — and separately keep a portable snapshot. Etsy's own "Download Your Reviews" (Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data → Take your data with you → Download Your Reviews) is free; open the file and verify whether it contains seller replies, original full-resolution photo URLs and listing URLs, because Etsy does not document the column list. If those fields are missing, you need another source for them before you change anything.

Run a five-row test import and keep the original file untouched. Match on Shopify product handles, not listing titles.

The legal bit

Under the FTC's Rule on Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, effective October 2024), reviews must be shown for the same product and from your own customers. Import per product, keep the Etsy attribution visible, and do not filter an archive down to only five-star reviews if that would misrepresent the overall rating.

Frequently asked questions

When did Judge.me add its Etsy review sync?

Judge.me shipped the free native Etsy → Shopify sync in July 2025. It connects your own Etsy shop, syncs on a schedule, includes photos and marks reviews verified.

Why are some of my Etsy reviews missing in Judge.me?

The sync covers written reviews on active listings. Rating-only reviews and anything on deactivated, sold-out, draft or expired listings fall outside it, as do seller replies.

Can I export a CSV from Judge.me’s Etsy sync?

No. The reviews live inside the app and the sync does not produce a portable file. Keep a separate archive from Etsy’s own Download Your Reviews.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Etsy Review Studio (independent browser extension)

What the extension does

It reads publicly visible review pages from the Etsy tab you have open and generates a CSV file on your computer. You choose when to run it and when to save the file.

What is stored, and where

Collected review records and preferences are cached only in chrome.storage.local on your own device. Extraction and CSV generation happen entirely in the browser. We operate no backend for review data, and no review content is uploaded, sold, shared or used for analytics.

Network requests

The extension loads the Etsy pages you are already viewing. If you activate Pro, only the license key is sent to Gumroad for verification. No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts are included in the extension or on this site.

Data you send us voluntarily

If you email support with a CSV template, that email and its attachment are used solely to answer your question and are not added to a mailing list.

Your controls

You can clear cached records from the extension popup, or remove the extension entirely — doing so deletes its local storage. There is no account to delete.
